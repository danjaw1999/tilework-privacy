Who is responsible

Tilework is made by JaworIT Daniel Jaworski, the data controller. Write to kontakt@jaworit.pl with any privacy question.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • puzzles you have started: your marks or path, the time, hints and checks used;
  • the results of each day's set, your streak, statistics, rank and badges;
  • which Play-mode puzzles you have already had, so they don't come back too soon;
  • settings: language, sound, haptics, board options, whether the reminder is on and its hour;
  • small counters: when and how often the app offered the reminder, the last day you finished a set, when the last full-screen ad was shown;
  • whether Premium is active;
  • a spare copy of the save and, if a save ever cannot be read, that unreadable copy, so nothing is lost.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes it. Tilework contains no analytics and no crash-reporting tools, and it does not show or link to other apps.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free game shows a banner under the board, now and then a full-screen ad when you leave a solved puzzle, and videos that you start yourself for an extra hint, check or streak save. The ads come from Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When an ad is requested, the SDK may collect and send to Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone, only if you allow tracking; the Android advertising ID) and the app set ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps;
  • diagnostic and performance data, such as launch times and SDK errors.

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. It is encrypted in transit. More: how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent to ads

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form (UMP) appears when the app starts, before any ad is requested. You can accept personalised ads, refuse them or choose in detail; without consent the ads are not personalised. Change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy › Ad privacy choices (the row is there wherever the consent form applies). The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) or, for non-personalised ads and fraud prevention, our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Tracking on iPhone

Right after the consent form (or at the first start, where there is none), iOS asks whether Tilework may track you. Google receives the IDFA only if you allow it, and every puzzle plays the same if you don't. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete the advertising ID in the system settings.

Daily reminder

The reminder is a local notification that your phone schedules itself: no push service, no token, nothing sent anywhere. It is on by default, but the system only asks for permission after you tap "Yes, remind me" in the app (offered after you solve a puzzle) or turn it on in Settings. On iPhone, earlier reminders may arrive quietly in Notification Center. You get at most one a day, at the hour you choose, none on days you have finished the set, and they stop after a week if you don't open the app. Switch it off in the app's Settings or in the system settings.

Premium

Premium is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), under their privacy policies. The app only learns whether the purchase is active, keeps that on your phone and asks the store again at launch, so a refund or a restore is reflected. We never see your payment details.

Children

Tilework is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has given us data, write to us and we will help.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict data and to object. We hold no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to hand over or delete; the data on your phone is yours to delete. For ad data you can withdraw consent in the app, reset your advertising ID or use Google's tools. You may also complain to a data protection authority; in Poland that is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Changes

A new version of this policy will be published on this page with a new date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app or in the update notes.
